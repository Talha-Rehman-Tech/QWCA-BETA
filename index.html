Allowable Deductions from Rental Income (Section 15A)

When filing annual income tax returns, landlords can legally subtract allowable expenses from gross rent to arrive at net taxable property income:

Section 15A
Statutory Repairs Allowance Flat 20%

A mandatory statutory deduction of 20% of gross rent allowed to every individual/AOP landlord. No receipts, invoices, or proof required.

Local & Property Taxes Actual Paid

Local rates, provincial property tax, municipal charges, and ground rent paid during the tax year are fully deductible.

Loan Mark-up / Interest Actual Paid

Profit or markup paid on loans borrowed from financial institutions (HBFC, scheduled banks) to acquire, construct, or renovate the property.

Collection & Administration Up to 4%

Administrative, rent collection, and legal expenses incurred to defend title, capped at a maximum of 4% of gross rental receipts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to frequent questions about our services and advisory engagements.

What services does QWCA provide?

QWCA provides a comprehensive suite of professional services, including corporate and individual taxation, statutory audit and assurance, SECP corporate secretarial compliance, accounting operations, and Special Economic Zone (SEZ) advisory.

How can I arrange a consultation?

You can schedule a consultation by calling our Faisalabad office at +92-41-5392192 or by submitting the contact form below. Our partners will review your requirements and respond promptly.

What should I bring to a consultation?

Please bring any documentation relevant to your enquiry, such as your prior years' tax returns, withholding certificates, bank statements, audited financial reports, or SECP incorporation certificates.

Can you help with tax notices and compliance?

Yes. We regularly represent clients before the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) and Punjab Revenue Authority (PRA), handling audit notices, show-cause notices, monitoring proceedings, and appellate representation.

When are my tax returns due?

Statutory deadlines depend on your taxpayer category. Salaried individuals and standard businesses generally file annual returns by September 30, whereas companies follow designated fiscal closing schedules. Please consult our Tax Calendar or contact our advisory team for specific dates.
